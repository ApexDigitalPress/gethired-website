Where most applicants get stuck

The job posting says "experience preferred." You have none. Now what?

Most people applying to customer service, chat support, or BPO roles for the first time hit the same three walls: they don't know how to talk about experience they don't think they have, they freeze on interview questions about handling angry customers, and once they're hired, the first month feels like drowning. None of that means you're not cut out for it — it means nobody handed you a plan yet.
